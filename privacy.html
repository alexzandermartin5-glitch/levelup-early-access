We do not sell your data, run third-party ad tracking, use an advertising ID, or collect precise location. Analytics is anonymous and stays off unless you turn it on.

Age requirements

You must meet the minimum age for your region to use LevelUp: 16 in the EU/EEA/UK (GDPR), 13 in the US and elsewhere (COPPA). A first-run age gate enforces this; under-age users cannot create an account.

Third parties (sub-processors)

  • Supabase — authentication + encrypted cloud storage of your game state (only if you create an account). Protected by Row-Level Security.
  • Open Food Facts — receives only a scanned barcode number, no personal data.
  • PostHog — anonymous product analytics, only after you opt in.

Data retention

  • Local data persists on your device until you delete it or uninstall.
  • Cloud data (if you signed up) persists until you delete your account.
  • Deleting your account removes local data immediately and cloud data via server-side erasure.

Your rights

At any time, from Settings in the app, you can:

  • Access / port your data — “Export my data” produces a portable JSON file.
  • Erase everything — “Delete my account & all data” wipes device + cloud data.
  • Withdraw analytics consent — toggle analytics off anytime.

EU/UK users also have the right to rectification, restriction, objection, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. California residents have the rights to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of “sale/sharing” — we do not sell or share personal information. To exercise any right, use the in-app controls or email the privacy contact above.

Security

Passwords are salted and key-stretched (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256) on the local backend and bcrypt-hashed server-side on Supabase. Session tokens are stored in the device secure enclave (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). Cloud rows are isolated per user by Row-Level Security.

Changes to this policy

Material changes bump the in-app policy version and re-prompt you for consent.
